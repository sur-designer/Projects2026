IELTS Speaking Mock Test

A realistic, timed test in a video examiner room. The examiner asks each question out loud, the timings match the real exam, and you get band scores for all four official criteria.

Test format

  1. Part 1 · Interview (4–5 min): 6 questions about familiar topics. About 40 seconds per answer.
  2. Part 2 · Long turn (3–4 min): a cue card, 1 minute to prepare, then speak for up to 2 minutes.
  3. Part 3 · Discussion (4–5 min): 4 deeper questions linked to Part 2. About 1 minute per answer.

Tip: turn on your camera in the test room to practise eye contact and body language, like in a face-to-face test. Nothing is recorded or uploaded.
